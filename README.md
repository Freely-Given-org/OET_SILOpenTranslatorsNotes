# OET_SILOpenTranslatorsNotes
This is re-sharing our adaptation of [SIL Open Translator’s Notes](https://github.com/BibleAquifer/SILOpenTranslatorsNotes) to the [Open English Translation](https://github.com/Freely-Given-org/OpenEnglishTranslation--OET)

We are very grateful to [SIL](https://global.sil.org/) for generously making their *Translator’s Notes* freely available.

Understandably, their notes are based on other translations (especially the [BSB](https://bereanbibles.com/about-berean-study-bible/)), whereas our [Open English Translation](https://OpenEnglishTranslation.Bible) makes a number of different translation decisions (especially concerning people's names),
so we have adapted the translator’s note to be more relevant to the *OET*.

These adapted resources are then used on our [Open Bible Data](https://github.com/Freely-Given-org/OpenBibleData) site which is accessible [here](https://Freely-Given.org/OBD/par/).
